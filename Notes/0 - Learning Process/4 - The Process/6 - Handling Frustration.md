Frustration is an emotional response to an event, situation, or condition that occurs in the form of disappointment or powerlessness. We all have a frustration tolerance, some are higher, some are lower, a lower frustration tolerance person will tend to give up or break off quickly when unexpected resistance arises, or the expected success does not occur within a specific time. Frustration tolerance can be trained and developed. It is to your advantage to do so. frustration generally is caused by lack of resources, and for our course that is resource is generally information. Given the field we will have to find that information ourselves, if we lack the skills to find that information we may start to feel anxiety which calls us back to our discussion about our  [[3 - Comfort|Comfort Zone]] 
To grow our frustration tolerance, we need to consciously, but in a controlled way, place ourselves in situations where a particular frustration can be assumed. 
Remember frustration is temporary. Frustration will pass, but the experience we gain remains and over time we will grow our frustration tolerance.
