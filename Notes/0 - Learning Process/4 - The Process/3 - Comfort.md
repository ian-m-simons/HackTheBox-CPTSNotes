Comfort is an emotional state of a person's mind which has a strong influence on behavior, thinking, focus, attention, and the ability to concentrate. While too much discomfort can negatively impact performance so can too little discomfort. Imagine a bell curve where the X axis is stress levels and the Y axis is productivity 
think of your comfort zone as concentric circles, form the center to outer ring you have 
1. comfort zone
2. fear zone
3. learning zone
4. growth zone.

leaving your comfort zone starts scary, then yoeu start to learn, acquire new skills etc and then you grow conquering new objectives etc. 
As we learn our comfort zone will expand as well.
