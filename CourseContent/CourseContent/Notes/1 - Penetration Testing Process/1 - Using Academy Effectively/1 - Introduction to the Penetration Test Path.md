### Purpose 
This module is an intro to the penetration testing job role path. It should provide a big picture overview of everything and can be referred to throughout the rest of the path. It is intended for people trying to break in and people trying to upskill in their current field. 
### HTB Academy Learning Philosophy
"Learn by Doing" we  follow a risk-based approach with a heavy emphasis on hands-on learning and the legal and ethical use of our skills. We strive to teach students the why behind a vulnerability and how to discover, exploit, remediate, detect, and prevent the flaw to create well-rounded professionals who can pass this all-encompassing knowledge and mindset on to their current/future clients or employers to assist them in securing their people, technologies, and mission from modern cyber threats
### Ethical and Legal Considerations 
Remember that the law exists, almost everything we do here is in a controlled and legal environment. Remember active reconnaissance and beyond without the explicit consent of an organization in the form of a scope of work, signed by both parties is against the law and could lead to legal action criminal action being taken. 
If you really need to scratch that itch, you can get additional practice by joining bug bounty programs. 
When (not if) you get your first penetration testing job, do your due diligence on your company. It is rare, but some criminal organizations will pose as legitimate companies to recruit talent and then you're on the hook for any illegal actions you perform. Always stay in scope, when in doubt reach out and ask, and if anything changes get it in writing before continuing.

### Penetration Tester Path Syllabus
The learning path in general is formatted to follow a penetration test. We will be attacking an imaginary company called inlanefreight. After you finish this path, you should specialize. Keep increasing generalist knowledge, but move towards a specialist in some area. If the content is too challenging, the do the information security foundations skills path.
