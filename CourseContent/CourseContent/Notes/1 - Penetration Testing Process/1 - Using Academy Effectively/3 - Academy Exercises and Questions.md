### The Goal
the goal of all the questions you will find here are to link knowledge with practice, and thus create chains of association. Some questions may seem rough/imprecise but they are that way for a reason. The point is to force you to continue to learn and practice. 
### Asking for Help
asking for help won't hurt you, but be willing to put in the work on your end and ask more precise question. Frame your question well and provide context, this can help you solve things yourself and if not it will at least provide more information for those helping you and show you are putting in the effort yourself. At the end of the day work hard, learn how to ask for help, and pay it forward to others going through what you went through and you will go far in this industry
### Words of Wisdom
some of what we do here is going to be really hard if you don't have at least some knowledge. Who cares, go learn what you need yourself if you need more pre-req knowledge.
