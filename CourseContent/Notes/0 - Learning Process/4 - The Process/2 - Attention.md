Attention is the perception of a specific topic with a higher level of interest in order to gather specific data and information from it. Attention is influenced by your interests, needs, personal attitudes, beliefs, orientations, goals, and experiences. Concentration is the maintenance of our attention on a specific topic. As the topic of information security is vast, we will need to understand how to divide our attention. it can be useful to document your attention span. If you do ensure you also document 
- current emotional state (one word, ie happy sad, worried, anxious etc)
- the previous flow of the day so far (one word)
- place of work
- working hours
- duration
- sleep
- inserted breaks
- duration of breaks
- anything else relevant worth noting
once you understand how your attention span is behaving you can to some degree manipulate it, while a perfect 60 min attention span doesn't necessarily equal 20-20-20 on 3 different topics we might still be able to use that 60 min effectively switching between topics.
