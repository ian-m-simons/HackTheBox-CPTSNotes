### Fear
Fear comes from things which are new, we do not know, and cannot evaluate. Generally speaking we have two types of fear, dangerous situations, and interpreted fear. Interpreted fear acn also be imaginary fear. Imaginary fear is directed at events we imagine without consequences that we calculate. Imaginary fear is an emootional state that keeps us from having the best experiences and prevents us from moving forward on the desired path.
