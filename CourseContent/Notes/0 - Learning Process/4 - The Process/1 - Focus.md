Focus is a skill where all or nearly all of our thoughts, concentration and attention are on a single topic. It is the purposeful and deliberate alignment to a specific goal. Think of the person who goes to the gym to blow off steam. While they are exerting their body and mind to workout, the focus on the exercise causes the other stressors to fade into the subconscious while they are now able to calm their mind afterwards. Don't confuse focus and attention, attention is the momentum as it is happening right now. Consider the losing your keys, your focus is on finding the keys, the attention is on searching for the keys. To make it a little clearer, if we decided to text our wife to see if she knew where the keys our, our focus would still be on finding the keys, but the attention would be on texting our wife.
