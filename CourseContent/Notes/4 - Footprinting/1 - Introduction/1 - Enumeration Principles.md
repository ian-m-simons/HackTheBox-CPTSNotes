Enumeration is a loop in which we repeatedly gather information based on what data we have or have already discovered. This is where your general IT background really comes in handy. Take some time to get to understand the infrastructure, how it's set up etc, rather than just banging your head against a brick wall. Our goal during enumeration is NOT to get into the systems, but to find all the ways we could potentially get there. ask the following questions at first glance
- What can we see?
- What reasons can we have for seeing it?
- What Image does what we see create for us?
- What do we gain from it?
- How can we use it?
- What can we NOT see?
- What reasons can there be that we do not see?
- What image results for us from what we do not see?
familiarize yourself with the following principles
1. There is more than meets the eye. Consider all points of view
2. distinguish between what we see  and what we do not see
3. There are always ways to gain mor information. Understand the target
