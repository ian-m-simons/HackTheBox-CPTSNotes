the goal of footprinting is to examine individual services and attempt to obtain as much information as possible from them
this module will cover 
- Enumeration principles
- Infrastructure-based enumeration
- FTP
- SMB
- NFS
- DNS
- SMTP
- IMAP/POP3
- SNMP
- MySQL/MSSQL
- Oracle TNS
- IPMI
- Windows and Linux remote management protocols
