We will inevitably get stuck and need help. here are some resources
### Forum
[Hack The Box Forum](https://forum.hackthebox.com/) is great for discussing live and retired boxes and challenges. on live boxes there are no spoilers or clear indications on how to exploit the box.
### Discord
There is a Hack the box discord which is worth joining, make sure you read the server rules to avoid getting in trouble.
### Asking Questions Effectively
when asking questions ensure you include:
1. What point of the box/challenge are we stuck at (ie user/root)
2. What steps have we taken so far to get to the point we are at?
3. What step are we failing at, and what have we done to resolve our issue?
4. Always try to be very specific on what we need help on, rather than asking for general help

ensure that you NEVER
1. Give away spoilers, or clear indications that may spoil the machine for other people
2. Be  vague in our description without providing enough details of what we have done
### Answering Questions Effectively
When answering the questions
1. Be as spoiler-free as possible, and do not give direct instructions on how to complete the current step or the entire box
2. Give minor hints or tips that can lead to the right direction for completion, and do not give entire suggestions for completion
3. Share resources that we found helpful
4. Share tips on points we were getting stuck on
### Getting Technical Help
check  [HTB FAQ](https://help.hackthebox.com/), or submit a  [ticket](https://help.hackthebox.com/en/) as needed
