### Passive learning
Following the Learning Pyramid, we can see that by reading we will learn no more than maybe 10% and by watching demonstrations we may push this number up to 30%
### Active Learning 
Discussions will raise our learning to around 50%. practicing on our own will grow our learning to around 75%.
Remember that all information we gather has quality, some is helpful. However some will confuse us. We need learn how to discern what is helpful and what isn't  this comes from practice. 
Remember to periodically look back on the progress you have made for motivation. 
Remember to take breaks so you don't burn out.
