You can't learn everything, there's just too much. Instead we need teed to focus on principles, structures, and processes.
There are lots of learning focused communities which you can and should utilize. When interacting with people here remember there are generally two kinds of people.
1. people who don't know anything
2. people who think they don't know anything
Remember most people also do not know their actual knowledge/skill level. 
A better option than trying to become an expert at everything (this will fail) is to develop a research methodology. Before we can develop this methodology we need to learn and practice how to research things. 
Becoming a good penetration tester only comes through considerable practice. Consider the concepts of passive and active learning. passive learning is things like reading, where as active learning is doing. Think about reading blackhat python vs actually going through and writing out all the examples, playing with it and customizing it. 
Most people never truly learn how to learn. That is the goal of this module.
