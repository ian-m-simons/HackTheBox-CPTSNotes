Talent is a myth. Talent is the development of a way of approaching problems. Consider a person who has spent years learning to play the Mandolin. One day they decide to learn to play Bass or Guitar. They don't have skill on that instrument. **However** because of their time spent learning mandolin, to others they will be perceived as talented. If we extrapolate this, we realize that we can develop our thought patterns, thought processes, and thus, by extension, our talents.
